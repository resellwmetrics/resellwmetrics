FAQ

Questions resellers ask.

Are the calculators free?

Yes. The calculators currently available on this site are free to use.

Are marketplace fee estimates guaranteed?

No. Marketplace fees can vary by category, account, transaction, promotion and policy changes. Always verify the current fee schedule before relying on an estimate.

Does Resell W Metrics buy or sell my items?

No. It provides independent planning and calculation tools for resellers.

Does the site have live sold-comparable data?

Not currently. Pricing decisions should be checked against current marketplace data where available.
